Second time in El Chorro

November 3, 2026·3 mins

I’m back in El Chorro, Spain, for a 10 day climbing holiday. The last time was in March 2025, when I came solo and sent my first 6c+. This time I came with J and we booked a room at the Olive Branch again.

Weather #

Last time it rained so much that the whole place flooded and a lot of people left early. This time the forecast said sunny all week, which I was very happy about. What I forgot is that warm weather isn’t always good for climbing here, because most of the crags in El Chorro face south. End of October was still too hot for them, so we went to Dispelomalandia every single day, one of the very few shady crags in the area.

Getting around #

Last time I came by train and relied on people with cars to go grocery shopping or reach crags further away. This time J and I rented a car at the airport. We weren’t going to originally, but I’m so glad we did. We used it almost every day to switch crags to get to a shady one like Displomelandia and shop at the supermarket. (Most of the crags around the OB are south facing and very hot in the sun, highly recommend having a car if you’re going early season like end of Oct)

Last time I sent a 6c+, which is still the highest grade I’ve ever sent. I wanted to either beat that or push myself further and try a 7a. That didn’t happen but on this trip I tried harder routes more often, projected a 6c and learned a lot from the process.

Maybe the biggest progress was feeling more confident to try out a harder route like a 7a or a 6c/6c+. I’m used to climbing with people stronger than me who could put the draws in for me but J and I are around the same level, so we weren’t sure we could do every move on the routes we picked. But I brought my clip stick and tried the hard routes anyways without having to rely on other climbers to clip in harder routes for me.

My favourite spot this time was Buena Sombra in Dispelomalandia.The 7a I tried on top rope had very bouldery moves, overhangs and roofs, and I realised how weak I am on overhanging, bouldery climbing.

To sum up, I tried hard, spent more time projecting on harder climbs than in the past and tried not to top-rope too much. So all in all pretty happy with my progress even though 7a still felt like a far away goal to reach. Maybe start with sending a 7a indoors?

Bugs #

Last time was end of March and this time was end of October. I don’t remember being bitten by so many insects before, but my whole body is covered in bites that aren’t going away I tried covering up completely and still got bitten, so maybe worth noting for next time.

Note to self #

  • Do more core and finger strength training
  • Kilterboard training for overhangs
  • Find a 7a project near Innsbruck and spend more time projecting and trying harder routes
  • Spend more time outdoors (climbing outdoors is suuuuper different from indoor climbing)
