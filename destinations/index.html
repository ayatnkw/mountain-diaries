You'll love this if…

Kalymnos you want a sunny island with thousands of sport routes and a climber community.
Geyikbayırı you want cheap winter sun, tufa pockets, and a climber-camp community.
El Chorro you want sunny multi-pitch limestone and a quiet Andalusian village base.
Buoux you appreciate climbing history and precise, technical pocket-pulling.
Frankenjura you love steep pocket pulling and the birthplace of the modern redpoint.
Arco you want lakeside limestone with everything from slabs to alpine multi-pitch.
Verdon Gorge you want to rappel into a limestone canyon and commit to long exposed pitches.
Corsica you want to mix climbing with other outdoor adventures in a wild Mediterranean island.
Tenerife you want winter-sun volcanic rock with a volcano hike on your rest day.
Krabi/Tonsai you want to climb overhanging tufas above the sea and swim between burns, mix it with a backpacking trip.
Thakhek you want climbing to be part of a jungle backpacking adventure.
Elbsandstein you want to climb on Sandstone around Berlin.
Löbejün you want a quiet old quarry for a beginner-friendly local day out.
Mitake you want a scenic riverside bouldering day trip from Tokyo.
