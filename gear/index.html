Gear

A living document of the kit I carry for different activities. Treat this as a reference, not a packing checklist. Nothing here is sponsored; it’s just what I use.

Mountaineering #

Footwear & traction

  • Mountaineering boots (semi-automatic crampon-compatible, stiff sole, B-C Boot)
  • Crampons (matched to the boot, Petzel Vasak)
  • Approach shoes / trail runners (Hoka Speedgoat 7)
  • Socks (for both pairs of shoes)
  • Gaiters

Base layers

  • Thermal long top
  • Thermal long bottoms
  • T-shirt/No sleeves
  • Long-sleeve
  • Shorts
  • Underwear
  • Bra
  • Buff

Insulation & shells

  • Fleece
  • Light compact down jacket
  • Soft-shell trousers (alpine, long)
  • Rain jacket
  • Rain pants
  • Gloves (inner and outer)

Climbing & glacier kit

  • Harness
  • Helmet
  • Ice axe (general mountaineering length)
  • Ice screw
  • Rope, 50 m / 8.6 mm
  • 4 locking carabiners
  • 3 prusiks
  • 120 cm sling
  • Ice screws & pulley (for crevasse rescue)

Pack & carry

  • 30 L backpack (ideally 35–40 L)
  • Liner bag
  • Waterproof bag / drybags (waterproof everything)
  • Spare plastic bags and ziplocks

Navigation & electronics

  • Head torch (+ spare battery)
  • Watch
  • Power bank, cables

Food & water

  • Water + water bladder
  • Snacks (protein bars, cereal bars, Haribo)

Health & hygiene

  • Sunglasses (Category 4)
  • Sunscreen
  • Toiletries (toothbrush, toothpaste, small towel, lip balm, hair tie, hair clip, contact lenses, glasses)
  • First aid (paracetamol)
  • Tissues

Documents & money

  • Cash (hut fee + 50 EUR)
  • Alpine club card
  • ID and driver’s license

Trekking

  • Hiking poles

On my wishlist #

  • Petzl Micro Traction
  • Tibloc
  • Paper or downloaded map
  • Paper and pen
  • Breathable headband
  • Quick-dry t-shirt
  • Smaller power bank
  • Spare phone battery
  • Lighter camera + camera clip
  • Insta360
  • Sleeping pill
  • Repair / fixing tape

What I’ve stopped bringing #

  • Books — the huts have plenty.

Before you go #

  • Put the sunscreen in a plastic bag.
  • Download the map before you leave.
  • Check the weather.
  • Call the hut.
  • Pack anti-tick insect spray.
